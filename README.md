# Shield_Insurance-Business_Performance_Dashboard-Power-BI


##  Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Business Performance Dashboard** developed for the **insurance domain**, focusing on **Shield Insurance**. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of customer growth, revenue trends, sales channel performance, age-group behavior, and estimated settlement exposure.

The objective of this project is to enable **data-driven decision-making** by bringing key business metrics and trends into a single, easy-to-use analytical view.

##  Business Objectives
- Monitor overall **customer and revenue performance**
- Identify **high-value customer segments** by age group and city
- Analyze **sales mode effectiveness** (Offline vs Online)
- Track **month-over-month trends** and detect performance spikes or drops
- Understand **estimated settlement exposure** across age groups
- Support **risk-aware and profitable growth decisions**

---

## Tools & Technologies Used
- **Excel** – Data cleaning, preparation, and initial validation  
- **Power BI** – Data modeling, DAX calculations, KPI creation, and dashboard development  

---

##  Project Workflow
1. **Data Preparation (Excel)**
   - Cleaned and validated raw datasets
   - Ensured data consistency and correct formatting

2. **Data Modeling (Power BI)**
   - Created relationships between fact and dimension tables
   - Designed a star-schema–based data model

3. **KPI & Measure Development**
   - Total Revenue  
   - Total Customers  
   - Daily Revenue Growth (DRG)  
   - Daily Customer Growth (DCG)  
   - Estimated Settlement Amount  
   - Month-over-Month comparisons  

4. **Dashboard Design & Visualization**
   - Built interactive visuals using slicers and filters
   - Designed pages for overall overview, sales mode analysis, and age group analysis
   - Focused on business-friendly layout and storytelling

---

## Dashboard Pages & Key Insights

### 1️⃣ Overall Business Overview
- Snapshot of total revenue, customers, and growth metrics
- Month-wise trends for customers and revenue
- City-wise and age-group-wise contribution analysis

### 2️⃣ Sales Mode Analysis
- Customer and revenue distribution across sales channels
- Monthly revenue trends by sales mode
- Age group preference by sales channel

### 3️⃣ Age Group Analysis
- Customer distribution by age group
- Estimated settlement exposure by age group
- Monthly growth trends by age segment
- Policy preference across age groups

---

## Business Recommendations
- Focus growth initiatives on the **31–40 age group**, the highest contributor to revenue and customers
- Strengthen the **Offline Agent channel** while gradually promoting digital adoption
- Accelerate **online sales channels** for younger and mid-age customers
- Review pricing and underwriting strategies for **higher-risk age groups**
- Optimize the policy portfolio based on **age-based preference insights**
- Analyze sudden growth spikes to identify repeatable and profitable drivers

---

## Business Impact
- Single, consolidated view of key business metrics
- Faster and more informed decision-making
- Improved revenue and sales channel optimization
- Better visibility into customer risk exposure
- Early identification of trends and anomalies
